## 6. What Is Functional Medicine?

Functional medicine uses a **systems-biology approach** — viewing the body as interconnected organs that must work in sync. Key principles:

- **Root cause focus:** Identifies *why* an imbalance exists rather than masking symptoms
- **Advanced testing:** Goes beyond standard labs to identify hidden imbalances
- **Personalized care:** Same symptoms can have different underlying causes in different patients
- **Preventive:** Focuses on maintaining health and preventing breakdown, not just treating disease
- **Lifestyle-first:** Diet, environment, emotional health, mindset are primary levers
- **Integrative:** Combines modern medical tools with natural healing modalities (diet, herbs, supplements)

**Contrast with conventional medicine:** Conventional medicine often treats the same symptom with the same medication across patients; functional medicine identifies unique imbalances per patient.

## 15. Key Medical Concepts Used in Practice

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| Gut Microbiome | Ecosystem of trillions of microbes in the digestive tract affecting digestion, immunity, and mental health |
| Dysbiosis | Imbalance between beneficial and harmful gut bacteria — linked to fatigue, brain fog, digestive issues |
| Leaky Gut (Intestinal Permeability) | Compromised gut lining allowing undigested particles into the bloodstream, triggering inflammation |
| SIBO | Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth — bacteria migrate to small intestine, ferment carbs, produce gas that pushes acid up (common GERD driver) |
| Senescence / Zombie Cells | Aged, dysfunctional cells that stop dividing but don't die; secrete inflammatory signals; targeted in longevity protocol |
| Gut-Brain Axis | Two-way communication between gut and brain via nerves, hormones, and gut microbes |
| Prebiotics | Foods/fibers that feed beneficial gut bacteria |
| Probiotics | Live beneficial bacteria introduced via fermented foods or supplements |
| Advanced Lab Testing | Goes beyond standard panels — includes stool microbiome analysis, genetic testing, heavy metals, food sensitivity, hormone panels |
| Functional Medicine | Systems-biology medicine focused on root-cause resolution and personalized care |
